AI Agents for Dummies

Zach Zimmerman

If you've been keeping up with anything tech-related, or even if you haven't, you might have heard the term "AI Agents" being thrown around.

This may sound like yet another brand-new technology threatening to leave you and your business in the dust, but I promise it's not. I'm going to explain as simply as possible what an AI Agent is, and how you can leverage it to accelerate your own business and keep up with modern software infrastructure.

What is an AI Agent?

Let's answer the obvious question first. What is an AI Agent? Long story short, it's an AI that is meant to carry out a specific task in a business workflow. Examples include writing an email, summarizing reports, generating reports, etc. Short story long, AI Agents are what happen when you provide additional context to assist an LLM on how to interpret prompts and instruct it how to perform tasks.

If that sounds too technical for you, don't worry about it. In fact, forget I said it.
